How these indicators are calculated

Attempted cases have been reviewed or moved beyond submission. Not attempted cases have no review date and remain submitted (including legacy routing states). Open includes solved cases awaiting closure and excludes closed or rejected cases. Not started includes accepted or assigned cases with no recorded action start. Age is elapsed days since submission; pending excludes solved, closed and rejected cases. Resolution rate includes solved and closed cases. Average resolution uses the solve date, or the close date where no solve date exists; no recorded completion is shown as “—”. Summary cards filter the case list; the form and chart links filter all reports.
